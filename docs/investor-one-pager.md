# Resonance ~ _know what your audience will love, before you post_

**Pre-seed · raising $600K** · AI content-resonance prediction for creators & brands

## Problem

Creators and brands publish **billions of posts a year on gut feel**, and most underperform.
Feedback only arrives _after_ publishing, when it's too late - and the only way to test content
in advance (lab-based audience/neuromarketing panels) is slow, costs tens of thousands, and is
reserved for big brands. Nobody can reliably answer the one question that matters before hitting
"post": **will this actually resonate?**

## Solution

**Resonance** is a mobile app: upload an image, video (reel/post), or audio clip and get an
instant **Resonance Score (0–100)** plus concrete fixes - _before_ you publish. Predictions are
**grounded in neuroscience** and **personalized to your actual audience**, not a generic average.

**Two ways in.** A solo creator checks a reel before posting. A **brand's marketing team
pre-tests a new commercial** - say a beverage company with a 30-second spot: compare edits, find
the weakest three seconds, and forecast audience response **before committing a seven-figure media
budget**. Same engine turns post-launch guesswork into pre-launch certainty; for brands, every
point of wasted ad spend avoided is the ROI.

## Why it works - and why it's defensible

1. **Proven science.** We build on **TRIBE**, Meta FAIR's trimodal brain-encoding model that
   **won the Algonauts 2025 challenge against 260+ teams**, predicting how the human cortex
   responds to video + audio + speech. It turns any clip into fine-grained, per-segment
   neuro-response features.
2. **Calibrated to reality.** Raw brain-response alone isn't engagement - and we don't pretend it
   is. When users **connect their Instagram / TikTok / YouTube accounts**, we train a proprietary
   model that maps those neuro-features to **their real post performance**. (Independent research
   confirms this is the right path: a _naive global_ brain signal fails, but **region-specific,
   segment-level features calibrated on real behavioral data** is exactly what's needed.)
3. **Data flywheel = moat.** Every connected account and every post outcome becomes new labeled
   training data → a better, more personalized model that competitors without our creator data
   cannot replicate.

## Product

- **Resonance Score** per asset, pre-post.
- **Engagement curve over the timeline** - _"your hook lands at 0:02, attention dips at 0:07 -
  trim it."_ Actionable, and demoable today from the model's per-segment output.
- **A/B variants** - upload 2–3 cuts/thumbnails, get the predicted winner.
- **Audience personalization** from connected accounts.
- **Actionable tips** - hook, ideal length, caption/hashtags, posting time.
- **Brand & agency dashboard + API** - the monetization surface.
- **Advisory for high-stakes launches (enterprise)** - the model's data-driven insight paired with
  human subject-matter experts to guide seven-figure creative decisions.

## Business model

**Usage-based credits** - free trial credits, then creators buy packs and brands/agencies buy
volume + API. Pricing tracks GPU inference cost, and **gross margin expands** as our lighter
proprietary model replaces the heavy MVP stack. A premium **enterprise advisory tier** - AI insight plus expert guidance for high-stakes campaigns - lifts ACV on the largest accounts.

### Why now

Multimodal foundation models just matured (V-JEPA 2, Llama 3.2, Wav2Vec-BERT), and **Algonauts
2025 put lab-grade brain modeling in software** for the first time - while short-form video
dominates attention and GPU inference is finally cheap enough to score one clip at a time.

### Competition & moat

Post-hoc dashboards (Hootsuite, Sprout) describe the past; heuristic tools (vidIQ, TubeBuddy)
guess from history; AI tools (Captions, Opus) _generate_ but don't predict outcomes; neuromarketing
(Nielsen) is lab-bound and enterprise-only. **None predict pre-post, personalized, neuro-grounded
resonance.** Our moat is the model **plus** the proprietary per-creator engagement data flywheel.
