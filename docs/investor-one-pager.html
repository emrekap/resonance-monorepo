AI content-resonance prediction for creators & brands · resonance

Problem

Creators and brands publish billions of posts a year on gut feel, and most underperform. Feedback arrives only after publishing. The one way to test content in advance - lab-based audience/neuromarketing panels - is slow, costs tens of thousands, and is reserved for big brands. Nobody can answer the question that matters before hitting "post": will this actually resonate?

Solution

A mobile app: upload an image, video (reel/post), or audio and get an instant Resonance Score (0–100) plus concrete fixes - before you publish. Predictions are grounded in neuroscience and personalized to your real audience, not a generic average.

Two ways in. A creator checks a reel before posting. A brand's marketing team pre-tests a new commercial - e.g. a beverage company with a 30-second spot: compare edits, find the weakest three seconds, forecast response before a seven-figure media buy. For brands, every point of wasted ad spend avoided is the ROI.

Why it works - & why it's defensible

  1. Proven science. Built on TRIBE, Meta FAIR's trimodal brain-encoder that won the Algonauts 2025 challenge over 260+ teams - turning any clip into fine-grained, per-segment neuro-response features.
  2. Calibrated to reality. Raw brain-response isn't engagement, and we don't pretend it is. Users connect Instagram / TikTok / YouTube; we train a proprietary model mapping neuro-features to their real post performance. Independent research confirms the path: a naive global signal fails, but region-specific, segment-level features calibrated on real data is exactly what's needed.
  3. Data flywheel = moat. Every connected account & post outcome becomes labeled training data → a better, more personalized model rivals can't replicate.

Product

  • Resonance Score per asset, pre-post.
  • Engagement curve over the timeline - "hook lands at 0:02, dip at 0:07 - trim it."
  • A/B variants - pick the predicted winner from 2–3 cuts/thumbnails.
  • Audience personalization from connected accounts.
  • Tips - hook, length, caption/hashtags, posting time.
  • Brand/agency dashboard + API - the monetization surface.
  • Advisory for high-stakes launches (enterprise) - AI insight + human subject-matter experts to guide seven-figure creative decisions.

None predict pre-post, personalized, neuro-grounded resonance. Moat = model + proprietary per-creator data flywheel.
